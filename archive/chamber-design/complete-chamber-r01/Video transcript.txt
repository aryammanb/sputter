3:58 Bent shutter and exact drop
The shutter is a single one millimetre sheet with two ninety degree bends, each with a two millimetre inside radius. 4:39 Shutter rotation and clearance checks
The shutter pivot is sixty five millimetres left of the gun axis. Turning the top thumb screw rotates the whole bent sheet. Closed, the blade is centred over the sample. Open, it rotates eighty degrees toward the rear. The open blade clears the sample edge by about six point six millimetres in the depth direction. The minimum sampled wall clearance is about two point nine. We checked eighty one shutter positions, at one degree intervals, against the chamber internals and the gun at seventy five, one hundred, and one hundred and fifty millimetre gaps. No positive volume interference was found in those checks. This is nominal CAD clearance; fabrication tolerances and a physical stop still need detailing.

5:21 What is fixed and what needs verification
The delivered files contain the complete assembly, open and closed views, individual principal parts, and a developed shutter blank. The blank uses a neutral axis K factor of zero point four two; the fabricator must adjust it for actual tooling. The shutter screw is represented as one quarter twenty eight, with a six point eight roof clearance hole. McMaster head details and the rotating O ring seal are reference envelopes, not a qualified feedthrough. The gun mounting bore, door and viewport seals, glass rating, and chamber pressure strength must also be verified before fabrication. The model now establishes the complete arrangement and its nominal dimensions, while keeping those remaining engineering decisions explicit.

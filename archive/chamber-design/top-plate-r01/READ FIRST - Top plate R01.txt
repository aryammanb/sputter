TOP PLATE R01 - PRELIMINARY INTERFACE LAYOUT
24 November 2026

Basis: 200 x 200 x 300 internal chamber; front access door; gun on top;
ISO63 pump underneath; left wall blank; three KF25 ports each on rear/right.

Proposed roof: 220 x 220 x 10 mm stainless blank with central 27 mm bore.
Thickness and 10 mm per-side overhang are layout assumptions, not approved
vacuum structural sizing or a final wall-joint detail. No roof bolt pattern
or perimeter seal is specified; a welded roof is the initial concept.
The neck allowance is OD33 / ID27 / 20 mm above the roof, to keep stack low.
It is NOT a finished KF25 flange. The flange and seal stack are not modeled.

REFERENCE / PROCUREMENT
Use Lesker QF25XVC100 as reference. Purchase selected eBay item 198508082866.
https://www.lesker.com/flanges/adapters-fittings-quickconnects/part/qf25xvc100
https://www.ebay.com/itm/198508082866
Lesker lists 25.4 mm tube OD, 39.88 mm flange OD, A25.9 and B50.8.
Do not interpret A/B without the controlled drawing. The diagram uses a
schematic adapter envelope, not supplier CAD. eBay dimensions are unconfirmed.

IMPORTANT COMPATIBILITY ISSUE
The 25.4 mm tube must pass the adapter, seal/centering ring, mating flange
and roof. Standard KF25 is NOT assurance of a 25.4 mm through bore.
Lesker QF25-100-SRV lists A23.6/B26; the actual cross section must be checked.
https://www.lesker.com/flanges/hardware-kf-centeringrings/part/qf25-100-srv
Standard KF25 weld stubs also have smaller bores (e.g. 22.225 mm).
https://www.lesker.com/newweb/flanges/flanges_kf_weld.cfm?pgid=weld3
Do not order a standard KF25 mating stack assuming it passes our tube.
Obtain confirmation of a complete 25.4 mm pass-through stack. If unavailable,
revise the chamber adapter selection and mating connection before fabrication.
Do not machine an off-the-shelf seal ring without an engineered seal review.

ASSEMBLY
Bring gun into chamber through the front door. Feed its service end upward
through roof and gland; the approximately 102 mm head remains inside.
Loosen/reposition only when vented and supported. Check the actual coupling
length, tube-end details and remaining sealing engagement over 75 mm proposed
travel before claiming a 75-150 mm target/substrate range is attainable.
Provide a deliberate ground bond; the tube O-ring is not an electrical bond.

DELIVERABLE SCOPE
STEP models cover roof and neck allowance only; no gun or supplier adapter CAD.
Geometry checks verify simple solid validity, bore clearance and reimport.
They do not verify structural, sealing or full gun-assembly compatibility.

IGNITION PRESSURE ASSESSMENT
Owner requested50mTorr ignition, then asked whether approximately42mTorr
would typically suffice. Engineering assessment:42mTorr (5.60Pa) is a
reasonable Ar magnetron ignition trial pressure, not a guaranteed strike
threshold for this custom gun.50mTorr is not a universal minimum.
Lesker's source-ignition diagnostic guidance recommends Ar pressure>10mTorr;
UCSB's operating experience on its AJA system gives10 or30mTorr ignition
examples followed by reduction to3mTorr. These establish plausibility, not
performance certification of this project's gun or supply.
Ignition also depends on available voltage/power, RF matching if applicable,
magnetic field and electrode/shield geometry, target condition and gas purity.
Do not infer that more pressure will always cure failure to ignite.

CALCULATED PRESSURES FOR THE SELECTED RANGE
Retain12L gross chamber, EXT70/EXDC80 and INDOVAC HV2-150 project basis.
Current layout remains unthrottled; no new valve is selected here.
At200sccm actual Ar, referenced to0degC/1013.25mbar, chamber gas at20degC:
Q=0.018124*200=3.6248mbar L/s; p=Q/S_eff, neglecting background gas load.
S_eff20L/s ->135.94mTorr;40L/s ->67.97mTorr;65L/s ->41.83mTorr.
Thus42mTorr is one illustrative case, not a measured pressure or assured
maximum.65L/s is based on an N2 rating; actual Ar speed remains unverified.
For50mTorr at200sccm, S_eff must be about54.4L/s or less under these assumptions.
Full-scale flow must not be commanded without established pump-load limits.
The200sccm instrument may reach the50mTorr target directly if installed speed
is sufficiently low, and may ignite plasma below50mTorr if the source permits.
If tests show inadequate ignition pressure, assess controlled chamber-side
throttling before changing MFC range; this would require separate engineering.

LOW-FLOW AND INTERFACE CHECKS
Verify supplied MFC's stable control and accuracy at intended deposition flow,
not only200sccm full scale. For illustration, an instrument rated2-100%FS
and+/-1%FS would provide4-200sccm control and+/-2sccm absolute accuracy.
Those figures are not established specifications of the owner's unidentified MFC.
Confirm actual VCR/tube interfaces, flow direction, mounting envelope, electrical
supply/pinout and control/readback before updating the CAD or control wiring.

COMMISSIONING BASIS
Establish pressure-versus-flow using a validated Ar-corrected pressure gauge
or suitable capacitance diaphragm gauge. Verify permissible Ar throughput,
cooling, foreline pressure, speed and drive load for the actual pump/EXDC80.
200sccm capacity alone does not establish a permissible operating gas load.
Validate ignition and subsequent reduction to the chosen deposition pressure
with pump/cooling permits and source protection active. Do not automatically
operate at full flow when lower flow already reaches the ignition pressure.
Ignition setpoint, power, duration and deposition recipe remain to commission.

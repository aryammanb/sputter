OWNER REQUIREMENT AND RECOMMENDATION
The owner specifies50 mTorr (6.666 Pa,0.06666 mbar) for plasma ignition.
The previous0-50 sccm recommendation covered an assumed deposition window
only; it does not reliably cover this ignition requirement with direct pumping.
Ignition at50 mTorr is a requested design target, not a demonstrated recipe.

For the CURRENT unthrottled chamber-to-EXT70 layout, provisionally specify
one normally closed, argon-calibrated0-300 sccm MFC. Release for purchase and
operation depends on installed Ar pumping performance and confirmation of
permissible Ar throughput for the actual EXT70/EXDC80/cooling/backing system.
300 sccm is a sizing allowance, not an approved pump operating flow.
Do not specify a generic SEC-E440J300 sccm solely because its body fits CAD:
its low-flow control and accuracy may be unsuitable for deposition.

CALCULATION
Use chamber effective speed, not nominal pump speed: p*S_eff = Q_Ar+Q_bg.
For Ar flow referenced to0degC/1013.25mbar and chamber gas at20degC:
Q_Ar [mbar L/s] =0.018124*F [sccm].
At50mTorr, neglecting background gas load:
Effective chamber speed       Required Ar flow
20 L/s                       73.56 sccm
40 L/s                      147.12 sccm
65 L/s                      239.07 sccm
These are constant-speed sensitivity cases, NOT an Ar pump performance curve
or a certified speed bound. Speed/conductance can vary at ignition pressure.
A300sccm range gives about25% headroom over239sccm in the65L/s case.
A200sccm controller misses that case. A100sccm controller reaches50mTorr only
if S_eff is approximately27.2L/s or less under these assumptions.
The12L chamber controls response time, not the steady flow needed to hold50mTorr.
Standard gas reference temperature must match the supplied MFC certificate.

CHOOSING BETWEEN TWO ARCHITECTURES
A. Retain current direct pumping: use0-300sccm, with verified low-flow accuracy.
   If this gas load is outside pump/drive limits, this architecture is not
   established as suitable for the50mTorr requirement, regardless of MFC size.
B. Engineering alternative: a0-100sccm MFC plus a vacuum-rated adjustable
   throttle between chamber and turbo, with chamber-pressure feedback.
   At50mTorr,20sccm requires about5.44L/s effective chamber speed;50sccm
   requires13.59L/s;100sccm requires27.19L/s. Restricting inlet conductance
   can therefore raise chamber pressure with less gas throughput.
   This is a proposed layout change, not selected hardware or completed CAD.
   Check valve conductance range, ISO63 interfaces, height/clearances, seals,
   supports, contamination shielding and controlled reopening pressure transient.
   A foreline valve is not a substitute for a chamber-to-turbo throttle.
   Do not disconnect or close off the turbo's backing pump to raise pressure.
   Do not use an uncontrolled vent burst as an ignition-pressure controller.

PUMP LIMITS AND COMMISSIONING
Edwards Table3 lists different continuous inlet-pressure limits for water,
forced-air and natural cooling; Table10 requires active cooling for continuous
high gas throughput. These are not gas-specific throughput approval for this
used pump with its EXDC80. Verify the matching manuals/manufacturer limits.
Keep the distinctions between chamber pressure, turbo inlet pressure and
foreline pressure: a throttle produces a pressure difference across itself.
The selected backing pump's9m3/h displacement is not its speed at foreline
pressure. Verify its actual curve and the allowable turbo backing pressure.

Commission with validated chamber-pressure measurement suitable for50mTorr
and the lower deposition range, with Ar correction where applicable. A suitable
capacitance diaphragm gauge avoids gas-species sensitivity. First establish
pressure versus calibrated Ar flow with plasma off and confirm stable pumping,
cooling, rotor speed, foreline pressure, drive current and temperature. Establish
permitted ignition flow and duration from those limits; no arbitrary boost
duration or300sccm command is authorized by this sizing calculation.
Then validate plasma ignition at the requested pressure and transition under
pressure feedback to the eventual deposition recipe. Define overshoot limits,
failure-to-ignite timeout, and gas/power shutdown on lost pumping/cooling permits.
50mTorr does not guarantee ignition: power supply, matching, target/gap and
magnetic configuration also matter.

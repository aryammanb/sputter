This revision incorporates the refined gun compression fitting and the
selected simple pumping layout: chamber -> EXT70 -> one mechanical pump.
No high-vacuum isolation valve or separate roughing bypass is selected.
Full assemblies are named/color-coded STEP exchange files, not native
SolidWorks assemblies with mates. Component instances include compounds.

IMPORTANT INTERFACE CORRECTION
The standard EXT70 manual specifies DN16NW at the backing outlet, not KF25.
The requested KF25 hose is therefore connected with a KF16/KF25 adapter.
If the actual owned pump has a confirmed KF25 variant, omit that adapter and
adjust the termination. Mechanical-pump hose goes to SUCTION, not exhaust.

REMAINING ENGINEERING
Not a fabrication release. Existing gun RF/cooling/seal limitations and
chamber pressure-strength/viewport/gasket qualifications remain.
Exact purchased gauges, gauge orientation/stray-field limits, hose bend
radius, rated ISO claw clamps, fasteners and mounting loads need checking.
Cooling plumbing, electrical cables/controllers/interlocks, chamber venting,
oil-mist exhaust handling, regulator/cylinder and line beyond push fitting
are outside this mechanical-layout revision. The RV3 is not a purchase choice.

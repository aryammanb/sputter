METAL SCREW WITH CERAMIC ISOLATION - VISUAL CONCEPT

This local section uses the original Boden, Isolator and Abstandshalter shapes
cropped around one existing M3 screw. It is not a complete gun redesign.
The metal screw is retained; no PEEK, PTFE or ceramic screw is proposed.

Electrical arrangement:
LIVE: steel spacer, metal screw and small metal washer under the screw head.
GROUND: Boden. The chamber/body ground connection must remain independently verified.
INSULATION: alumina tube around the shank through Boden, and a separate alumina
washer between the live metal head washer and the grounded seating face.
The original main PTFE sealing ring remains between the grounded and live bodies.

Mechanical arrangement:
The metal screw carries tension. The alumina head washer carries local compressive
bearing load. The tube insulates the shank and does not replace the screw's threads.
The main PTFE ring still lies in the overall clamping stack, so its creep is not
eliminated. This concept specifically replaces the small PTFE screw insulators.

Concept dimensions, NOT released machining tolerances:
Ground counterbore enlarged from 8 to 12 mm diameter, original floor at Y=-68.
Alumina tube: OD5.6 ID3.3 length22; Y=-68 to -46.
Alumina washer: OD11.6 ID3.3 thickness2.5; Y=-70.5 to -68.
Metal washer: OD7 ID3.3 thickness0.5; Y=-71 to -70.5.
M3x35 screw underhead Y=-71, tip Y=-36, head bottom Y=-74.
Nominal entry into spacer is5 mm including entry chamfer; effective full-thread
engagement and strength must be checked. No thread helix is modeled.
This is not an off-the-shelf supplier part selection or a drop-in replacement.

Checks completed:
Seven valid solid bodies on STEP reimport. Only detected volume intersection is
the nominal screw shaft versus the original thread-minor bore, as expected for
unmodeled threads. Source geometry is unmodified except the local counterbore cut.

Before implementation:
Check all twelve fastener positions in the full assembly, remaining wall and
nearby holes, fits and tolerances, ceramic grade and surface finish, seat flatness,
edge relief and stress, torque/preload, thermal expansion and RF clearances.
Ceramic should not be treated as an unbreakable hard stop or used with uncontrolled
tightening. Protect exposed live heads and evaluate surface contamination paths.
There is no rated operating voltage, RF power or ceramic stress result in this model.

Video views are explanatory: parts are separated sideways in the exploded view;
that movement is not a physically verified installation/removal sequence.

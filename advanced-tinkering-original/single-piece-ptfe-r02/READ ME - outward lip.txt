R02 SINGLE MACHINED PTFE PART WITH OUTWARD LIP

The shank sleeve, under-head bearing shoulder, screw-head guard wall and outward
lip are one continuous solid. The metal M3x35 head clamps the integral shoulder
against the proposed Boden pocket floor. There is no separate guard fastener.

In the local axial section the guard emerges from the hole, then turns radially
outward over the surrounding grounded face: the second L requested by the user.

TWELVE-STACK MAGNETRON: MECHANICAL ACCOMMODATION STUDY
23 November 2026

RESULT
The proposed magnet pack can be accommodated within the current external gun
envelope. It requires a coordinated local revision of THREE machined parts:
the copper cooling body, copper backplate and steel yoke. It is more than a
hole-size change, but does not require a new target clamp, spacer, main
insulator, base, external shields, service tube or RF connection in this study.
The two water seals and the upper coolant routing change too.

This is one workable nominal mechanical arrangement, not the only possible
layout or a fabrication release. Magnetic balance and thermal performance
have not been simulated. In particular, the revised outer pole radius must
be evaluated magnetically before it is frozen.

BASELINE
Latest combined R03 assembly, retaining the original Advanced Tinkering head.
129 solid component instances. Outer magnets: twelve D8x20 N45 rods on PCD50.
Centre: two stacked D12x10 N45 discs. Original source files remain unchanged.

PROPOSED PACK
Outer: 24 standard D6x10 N42 discs, paired into twelve D6x20 stacks.
Centre: two standard D20x10 N42 discs, giving a D20x20 stack.
Axial magnetization. Centre N toward target, outer S toward target.
Both discs in each stack point in the same magnetization direction.
All pole tips remain Y=-2.5, with the yoke support face at Y=-22.5.
Outer positions move from PCD50 to PCD58, retaining 30-degree spacing.
The area ratio is 12*6^2/20^2 = 1.08; this is NOT the operating flux ratio.
141 component instances result because twelve rods become 24 discs.

WHY THE EXISTING CENTRE REGION IS TOO SMALL
A D20 magnet is represented in a D21 clearance bore, radius10.5.
The existing coolant channel starts at radius11.05. Simply enlarging the bore
leaves only 0.55mm nominal copper between the bore and water channel.
The old inner gland occupies radii7.25..10.10: the D21 bore removes it.
The existing port counterbores have radius3.975 on axes X=+/-13.55; their
inner radial edge is9.575, so they also overlap the enlarged centre bore.
These are geometric conflicts, independent of whether the field is balanced.

PART-BY-PART CHANGES

1. COPPER COOLING BODY / KUEHLKOERPER
   - Centre blind well: D13 -> D21.
   - Twelve outer blind wells: D9 -> D6.4, PCD50 -> PCD58.
   - Coolant annulus: radii11.05..16.05 -> radii15.5..20.5.
   - Channel width5 and depth7 retained; volume increases with circumference.
   - Blind-well roof remains Y=-2; magnet tips remain0.5mm below it.
   - Outer profile, target seat and original fixing interfaces retained.
   - No overall head diameter or length increase.

2. COPPER BACKPLATE
   - Centre through well D21; twelve D6.4 through wells on PCD58.
   - Inner seal nominal free size: ID14.5x2 -> ID23x2.
   - Outer seal nominal free size: ID35x2 -> ID43x2.
   - Gland radial ranges:11.5..14.35 and21.5..24.35.
   - Existing nominal gland width2.85 and depth1.45 retained.
   - Coolant axes X=+/-13.55 -> +/-18, displacement4.45 per side.
   - D5 through passages and nominal D7.95 port counterbores retained.
   - OD71.5, thickness13.5, twelve fixing holes and yoke/live-terminal
     attachment locations retained.
   - Port and screw threads are represented by nominal bore envelopes.
     Exact fitting thread and engagement remain to be specified.
   - Two source0.25mm threaded-bore entry chamfers are omitted in this study
     solid. This accounts for a small increase in the nominal screw overlap;
     it is a modelling simplification, not a required functional change.

3. STEEL RETURN YOKE
   - Two D14 fitting-clearance holes move from X=+/-13.55 to +/-18.
   - OD71.5, thickness10 and attachment positions retained.
   - New holes leave1mm nominal steel between the centre pole footprint
     and fitting-clearance edge. Magnetic saturation/return flux need analysis.

4. MAGNETS AND WATER SEALS
   - Replace both magnet sizes and grades as listed above.
   - Replace the two water seals. Main head seal and main insulation seals
     retain their baseline geometry and positions.
   - Displayed water seals use illustrative compressed elliptical sections;
     material, gland fill, tolerances and seal reliability are not qualified.

5. COOLANT CONNECTIONS AND FLEXIBLE ROUTES
   - Existing reference fitting envelopes move4.45mm outward on each side.
   - Upper hose bends follow the moved fittings.
   - Lower routes remain X=+/-3 within the existing tube.
   - No change to hose nominal OD4/ID2.5 in the illustration.
   - Actual purchased fittings, hose specification and minimum bend radius
     remain unconfirmed. The geometry verifies these reference envelopes.

6. RETAINED PART GROUPS
   - Target and target retaining clamp; target mounting screws.
   - Grounded dark-space shield and shield tube.
   - Live spacer and main head seal.
   - Main PTFE insulator and its two seals.
   - R03 base, twelve one-piece PTFE cups and base fasteners.
   - KF16-style clamp, seal and centering components; service tube.
   - R02 RF shield clamp, ground carrier, live lug, screw, coax and guards.
   - Existing screw sizes and mounting patterns.

NOMINAL ROOM IN THE REVISED RADIAL STACK
Centre bore edge -> inner gland:1.00mm.
Inner gland -> water channel:1.15mm.
Water channel -> outer gland:1.00mm.
Outer gland -> nearest outer magnet well:1.45mm.
Outer magnet well -> backplate outside edge:3.55mm.
Centre bore -> coolant counterbore:3.525mm.
These are geometrical lands, not approved pressure-wall or seal-land minima.

VERIFICATION AND LIMITS
Read Geometry verification.json for STEP reimport validity, exact boolean
contact checks, channel obstruction and the baseline comparison of screw
contacts. Part change register.csv lists every proposed component instance.
All unchanged-component relationships are inherited from the existing R03
study; the new work checks interactions involving changed components.

The required next steps are magnetic field modelling, thermal/contact and
coolant analysis, actual fitting selection, seal/tolerance review and prototype
measurement. A successful mechanical fit does not establish a balanced field.

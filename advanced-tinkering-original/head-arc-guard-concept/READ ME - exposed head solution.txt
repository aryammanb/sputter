EXPOSED LIVE SCREW HEAD - GUARDED CONCEPT

Purpose: develop the screw-head/ground-pocket geometry identified by the user
as the reported arcing location. The earlier ceramic washer concept left the
side of the live head exposed. A material substitution alone did not address it.

Proposed arrangement:
1. Keep the metal M3x35 clamping screw and separate ceramic bearing insulation.
2. Add a machinable PTFE guard surrounding the live head and metal bearing washer.
3. Overlap the ceramic washer with the guard's upper lip, rather than leaving
   a straight side opening beside the live washer. The guard is not clamped by
   the screw and is not a structural fastener.
4. Add a removable PTFE threaded cap for the tool-access opening.
5. Vent the pocket to the chamber through an offset pump-out route. Do not
   intentionally trap atmospheric gas in a sealed screw-head pocket.

The conceptual guard uses an external M12x1 thread into a redesigned Boden
pocket. The cap uses a nominal M9x1 thread inside the guard. These are proposed
retention interfaces; thread helices, reliefs, fits, tolerances and tightening
limits have not been detailed. Their solids use nominal thread envelopes, with
expected overlapping volumes at those interfaces. They are not ready-to-machine
thread drawings or an off-the-shelf part specification.

Dimension study:
Original head pocket8mm. Proposed pocket: lower nominal M12x1 minor diameter
10.917, with12mm upper washer-seat diameter. Existing floor remains atY-68.
Ceramic shank sleeve OD5.6 ID3.3 length22, same conceptual location as before.
Ceramic washer OD8.8 ID3.3 thickness2.5; revised OD to fit the guard's overlapping lip.
Metal washer OD7 ID3.3 thickness0.5. Screw head underfaceY-71, bottomY-74.
Guard OD12; lower bore8, upper lip bore9. Guard extendsY-78 to-69.5.
Upper lip overlaps ceramic washer axially1mm; radial clearance0.1mm nominal.
This small nominal gap requires a toleranced thermal/assembly fit review.
Lip remains1.5mm below the grounded floor; main screw preload is not transferred
through the protective guard. Nominal guard thread engagement in body3.5mm.
Guard extends4mm beyond head/grounded face. Cap extends6mm beyond grounded face.
Cap head OD11.6 thickness2; spigot nominalM9x1 length2, endingY-76.
Cap top is2mm below lowest live head surface. These dimensions are not voltage ratings.
Two0.6mm intersecting bores form a90-degree pump-out route in the cap.
There is no straight-through driver hole to the screw; external driver slot is blind.

Model checks:
Nine valid solids on STEP reimport. Local intersections are only three nominal
thread interfaces: metal screw/live spacer, guard/Boden, cap/guard.
Guard and cap external envelopes are checked at all twelve source screw locations
against the original reconstruction, excluding Boden which requires new pockets.
See Guard envelope checks.json. This does not approve the new hole pattern,
remaining walls, thread strength, tool access or assembly/removal procedure.

Electrical limits:
This changes exposed-head geometry and is intended to reduce the particular
discharge opportunity. It does not establish arc-free operation. Surface flashover,
field concentration at metal/insulator/gas junctions, RF dielectric heating and
conductive deposition remain relevant. No field or plasma calculation is supplied.
The protective enclosure is not a vacuum seal. Confirm which spaces communicate
with chamber vacuum before manufacture; do not drill through the pressure boundary.
Final dimensions require actual gas, pressure range during pumpdown and operation,
peak RF voltage/current and DC bias. Power alone is insufficient to establish them.
Evaluate retention after thermal cycling and inspect/replace contaminated guards.
Main PTFE sealing-ring compression/creep remains a separate issue.

CURRENT MAGNETRON R03 - COMPLETE WALKTHROUGH ASSEMBLY
23 November2026

This is the latest combined mechanical study used for the narrated exploded video.
It retains the original Advanced Tinkering head and magnet arrangement and combines:
- R02 continuous coax termination with shield clamp, ground carrier and live lug;
- R02 25.4mm OD service tube with source-compatible smaller KF16 capture neck;
- twelve one-piece PTFE sleeves/shoulders/guards/outward lips from the latest proposal;
- twelve12mm head pockets in Boden while retaining the R02 blind ground anchor;
- twelve original M3x35 base screw shapes moved1mm rearward to seat on the new cups.

The assembly contains129 component instances, the same count as the single-coax
R02 assembly. Twenty-five instances changed: twelve cups, twelve screw placements,
and one Boden. Earlier assemblies and original source files remain unchanged.

FORMAT

VERIFICATION
All129 solids pass validity checks after STEP reimport. Total volume matches the
constructed assembly within the reported tolerance. New cups, changed base screws
and their interactions with other components were checked for volume interference.
The only twelve new intersections are nominal screw shafts in source thread-minor
bores, intentionally represented without thread helices. The twelve enlarged Boden
pockets are subtractive changes. Existing R02 thread/spring/contact allowances remain.
See Assembly verification.json and the R02 notes for the scope of these checks.

Original supplied machined shapes are retained except Boden's proposed pocket and
ground-anchor changes. The source cooling annulus, magnet wells, spacer, main PTFE
ring, yoke, target clamp and outer shields remain as reconstructed previously.
Magnets:12 rods diameter8x20, two discs diameter12x10, N45, outer pitch circle50.

OPERATING LIMITS
This video and CAD support inspection of the design. They do not validate operating
power, RF voltage/current, self-bias, coolant electrical isolation, cooling capacity,
magnet temperatures, leak rate or coating performance. Those remain engineering
and prototype verification tasks. The sliding chamber gland is not assumed to
provide the required mechanical retention or RF/protective-ground bond.

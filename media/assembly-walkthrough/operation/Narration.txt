Load the substrate
The tool coats a substrate with a thin film of target material. After venting, open the door and place the substrate on the stage. The shutter is parked for access. Close the door before evacuation.

Evacuate the chamber
The closed chamber is evacuated through the bottom connection, turbo and backing pump. This is the actual pumping route in the CAD. Pressure measurement determines when the next process step can begin.

Admit regulated argon
Regulated argon enters through the mass flow controller and the upper rear chamber port. Gas admission and pumping establish the process conditions. The flow controller meters gas; the gauges measure chamber pressure.

Shutter closed · target conditioning
R F power sustains an argon discharge. Argon ions strike the target and eject target material. With the shutter closed, the blade intercepts that material above the substrate. The target can condition before the sample is exposed.

Substrate shutter · opening
This top view temporarily hides the complete gun so the shutter movement is visible. Turning the offset shaft swings the bent blade away from the substrate. It opens the path from the source to the sample.

Shutter open · substrate coating
With the shutter parked, sputtered target material reaches the substrate and builds a thin film. The source remains above the fixed stage. The shutter controls the coating interval without moving either the substrate or the gun.

Close the shutter · end exposure
Turn the shaft back to bring the blade over the substrate. Closing the shutter ends direct exposure from the source. The source is then switched off before the controlled shutdown and vent sequence.

Vent, open & unload
After the source is off and the pumping system has completed its shutdown and vent sequence, open the door and remove the coated substrate. This completes the high-level cycle: load, evacuate, admit gas, condition, coat and unload.

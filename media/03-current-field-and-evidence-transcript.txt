Current target-plane field assessment
The original nonlinear magnetostatic model used a two millimetre nonmagnetic target and gave ninety five point one millitesla mean parallel field near its surface. That is an earlier target revision. This updated assessment samples the saved fine mesh solution a quarter millimetre above the current three point one seven five millimetre target. The calculated mean parallel field is eighty six point four three millitesla. The range is eighty two point nine six to eighty nine point five three, with a mean normal field zero contour radius of twenty point one nine millimetres. The changed target, copper and fittings have unit relative permeability in this model, and the magnets and yoke are unchanged. This supports resampling the existing solution. It is not a new mesh or nonlinear solve, and it does not establish new uncertainty bounds or experimental validation.

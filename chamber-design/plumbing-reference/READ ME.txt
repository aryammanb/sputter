This is a topology comparison, not an operating procedure. Option B does
not, by itself, establish that the EXT70 can remain running while its backing
connection is isolated. A validated sequence, pressure monitoring and
acceptable foreline holding conditions must be established first.
Do not connect an atmospheric chamber directly to a running turbo's foreline.

omitted. Gas-flow arrows point to pump suction, not to pump exhaust.

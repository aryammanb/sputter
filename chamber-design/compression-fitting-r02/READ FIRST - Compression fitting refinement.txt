The cap, body, follower and tube O-ring are separate named solids. The cap
and body have real matching helical geometry in this reference model.
The KF clamp has separate opening jaws, hinge pin, screw and wing nut.
The yellow cap index is a visual animation marker, not purchased hardware.

OPERATION SHOWN
1. Seat adapter KF face on chamber flange with separate KF face seal.
2. Close KF clamp and tighten wing nut; this fixes the body to the chamber.
3. Feed gun service tube upward through the loose compression cap.
4. Support gun and slide tube to chosen height.
5. Screw cap down the stationary body; its shoulder loads the follower,
   squeezing the tube O-ring against the tube. The tube does not rotate.
6. To readjust while vented, loosen the cap, reposition, and retighten.
   The KF connection stays assembled.

No torque, number of turns or seal squeeze in the animation is an assembly
instruction for the purchased fitting. Use its actual supplier instructions.
O-ring deformation preserves elliptical section area as a visual explanation;
it is not a seal or clamping-force calculation. The exact purchased fitting
may transfer compression through a different follower/seat arrangement.
Body and cap have zero positive-volume overlap in the assembled CAD check.

THE TWO SEALS ARE DIFFERENT
Tube O-ring: seals and grips around the smooth service tube.
KF face seal: seals between adapter flange and chamber flange.
The actual mating flange/centering ring must pass the25.4 tube. The earlier
standard-KF25 bore concern remains unresolved; the animation assumes a clear
through path and does not qualify an off-the-shelf centering-ring stack.

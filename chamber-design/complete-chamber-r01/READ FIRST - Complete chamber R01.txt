The two STEP states are alternatives, not two assemblies to combine.

SCOPE / REMAINING ENGINEERING
This is a dimensioned mechanical concept, not a fabrication release.
Wall/roof/door/glass pressure strength, deflection, buckling, weld design,
seal compression, hinge/latch selection and fastening details need review.
The existing gun's RF, cooling, seal and source-CAD limitations still apply.
No pump-down time, coating uniformity or process performance is established.
CAD clearance checks do not account for manufacturing tolerances or flexure.

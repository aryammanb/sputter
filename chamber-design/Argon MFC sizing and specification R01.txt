SIZING METHOD
At steady pressure: p * S_eff = Q_Ar + Q_background.
Thus required Ar flow depends on pressure and effective pumping speed at the
chamber. Vessel volume does not set steady-state flow. In molecular flow,
1/S_eff = 1/S_pump + 1/C, with C the intervening path conductance for Ar.
Conductance and pump speed can change with pressure; do not treat this simple
model as an installed performance curve.

For sccm referenced to 0 degC and 1013.25 mbar, at chamber gas temperature T:
Q_Ar [mbar L/s] = F [sccm] * 0.0168875 * T/273.15.
At T = 293.15 K (20 degC), Q_Ar = 0.018124 * F, approximately.
Neglecting background load:
F [sccm] = p [mbar] * S_eff [L/s] / 0.018124.
1 mTorr = 0.00133322 mbar = 0.133322 Pa.
At equal standard/chamber temperature, the usual 0.01689 factor applies.
Require the supplier's actual standard reference temperature on the certificate;
20/25 degC-referenced sccm must be converted consistently.

Sensitivity calculation, 20 degC chamber gas, negligible background load:
Chamber pressure       F at20 L/s     F at40 L/s     F at65 L/s
1 mTorr                 1.47 sccm      2.94 sccm      4.78 sccm
3 mTorr                 4.41 sccm      8.83 sccm     14.34 sccm
5 mTorr                 7.36 sccm     14.71 sccm     23.91 sccm
10 mTorr               14.71 sccm     29.42 sccm     47.81 sccm
20 mTorr               29.42 sccm     58.85 sccm     95.63 sccm
The 20/40/65 L/s values are sensitivity cases, not measured Ar speeds or a
certified bound. 65 L/s is a provisional high-speed case based on the N2 rating.
At50 sccm and65 L/s, calculated pressure is approximately10.46 mTorr.
The 50 sccm selection has little headroom at10 mTorr in that high-speed case.
If measurement or the Ar pump curve requires >50 sccm, select100 sccm or
engineer a throttle system; do not assume the current baffle is a throttle.

For the assumed20-65 L/s range, ideal pressure time constant V/S is
approximately0.18-0.60 s using12 L; a95% response is about3 time constants.
These are small-signal ideal gas estimates at constant speed, not a full
pump-down time, controller tuning, or a required wait time for deposition.
MFC response, gauge lag, gas lines, plasma and wall desorption add dynamics.

INTEGRATION AND COMMISSIONING
Gas route: Ar cylinder -> regulator -> suitable clean gas tubing -> MFC inlet
adapter -> MFC -> VCR/KF25 outlet adapter -> rear chamber gas port.
The existing6mm push fitting/tube are interface references; confirm pressure,
gas compatibility and cleanliness of actual purchased parts. Support MFC
independently and use the correct VCR gaskets.

MFC sets flow; gauge feedback is needed to know chamber pressure. With the
current unthrottled route, flow and pressure are coupled. Independent control
of both would require a deliberately engineered pumping conductance control.
Use an Ar-corrected, validated gauge in the selected range; a suitable capacitance
diaphragm gauge is preferable for gas-independent process pressure measurement.

Before accepting operating setpoints, check zero/closed flow, external leaks,
calibration, and pressure versus several known flows with plasma off. Subtract
the background gas-load contribution when inferring S_eff. Repeat process
checks with plasma on. Confirm cooling, foreline pressure, rotor speed and pump
temperature/current remain within the actual pump/EXDC80 manuals' limits.
An MFC's50 sccm full scale is not permission to run the pump continuously at
that flow. The cited pump table is not an Ar maximum-throughput certification
for the used pump/drive combination. Final permissible flow needs that check.
Inhibit gas and sputter power on loss of the required pumping/cooling permits;
define the actual trip limits during control-system engineering. Provide
independent gas isolation; MFC zero command is not a service isolation valve.

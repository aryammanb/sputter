COOLANT FITTING REVISION R06
24 November 2026

Selected fitting: 2 x SMC KQG2S04-M5, 4 mm tube OD, M5x0.8 male thread,
SUS316 metal parts and special FKM seals. Internal 2 mm hex installation.
Manufacturer envelope: OD9 mm, overall17.1 mm, installed projection14.1 mm.
US buying reference: https://www.smcpneumatics.com/KQG2S04-M5.html
Manufacturer catalog saved in ../coolant-fitting-selection.

CHANGES
- Copper backplate ports remain centred at native X +/-18 mm, Z0.
- Former nominal D7.95 counterbores and D5 passages are removed.
- New ports: D3 flow passages through, D4.2 tap drills5 mm deep from rear
  faceY-22.5. Callout: 2X M5x0.8-6H, full useful thread4 mm minimum.
- Flat gasket seating face retained around each port. Use the specified
  manufacturer's supplied M5 gasket and installation procedure; thread tape
  is not a substitute for the gasket seat.
- Existing D14 yoke openings retained: nominal radial clearance toOD9 body
  is2.5 mm. Fitting top is4.1 mm beyond the10 mm thick yoke face.
- Two fitting bodies and release rings replaced by catalogue envelope models.
  Hidden internal details are representative, not manufacturer CAD.
- Hose insertion segment extended to match the new fitting. Existing hose
  bends and4mmOD/2.5mmID retained; supplier bend-radius qualification remains.
- R05 aluminium3.175 mm target and clamp retained. Target-to-substrate
  adjustment and chamber layout unchanged.

SOLIDWORKS
Native copper backplate contains two cut-extrude features for passages and
pilot bores. M5 thread is specified by feature names/custom properties, not
helical tooth geometry. This is a machining specification candidate, not
an approved detailed fabrication drawing or a qualified seal design.
Native assembly components are fixed at the checked assembly positions.

CHECKS AND LIMITS
Changed-part Boolean checks found no new non-thread interference.
Fitting-to-tap-minor-bore overlap is expected nominal threaded engagement.
Existing yoke screw / RF terminal thread-envelope overlaps are retained.
Native imports are checked for body validity and volume agreement. Unchanged
curved imported parts can differ slightly between CAD kernels; baseline
volume tolerance0.1%, revised backplate0.01%, absolute tolerance0.02 mm3.
No zero-leakage or water-in-vacuum performance claim is made. Manufacturer
KQG2 vacuum-retention caveat remains. Pressurized coolant joints must be
qualified before fabrication/operation release.

SYSTEM CONTEXT

in the complete comparison STEP for continuity but is excluded from the
native SolidWorks build. It is not the selected INDOVAC HV2-150 pump;
replacement vendor geometry is still pending. Other unchanged purchased
component/chiller envelopes remain representative.
